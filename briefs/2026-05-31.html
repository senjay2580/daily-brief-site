行业内行课

Robotics / Industrial Automation

Physical AI is pulling robotics compute closer to factories and warehouses

Robotics coverage this cycle keeps converging on edge AI platforms, warehouse orchestration, and the shift from scripted automation toward systems that can perceive and adapt in production environments.

The practical constraint is no longer only robot hardware. Vendors are competing around compute stacks, safety validation, cybersecurity, and integration with existing warehouse or shop-floor systems.

行业思维：Robotics buyers should把 AI 能力放进安全标准、系统集成流程和责任边界里评估，而不是只看单点 demo。

Semiconductors / AI Chips

AI memory, chiplets, and energy efficiency are becoming the semiconductor battleground

Semiconductor results point to a market where AI data-center demand is pulling capital toward HBM, advanced packaging, chiplet integration, and ecosystem investments around Taiwan and leading foundries.

At the same time, policy and power constraints are reshaping the competitive map: export controls, China strategy, and energy use are increasingly part of chip architecture and supply-chain decisions.

行业思维：Semiconductor 竞争的关键壁垒正在转向系统级决策：内存带宽、封装、供应约束和每瓦成本要一起优化。

Semiconductors & AI Chips Weekly Briefing - May 29, 20262026 Global Semiconductor Industry Outlook - Deloitte
Cybersecurity / AI Security

AI security is shifting from awareness to continuous assurance

Cybersecurity sources emphasize that AI has become both a defensive accelerator and a new exposure layer: attackers can find weaknesses faster, while enterprises are deploying AI tools faster than governance can catch up.

The strongest pattern is the move from one-time review to continuous validation of AI tools, cloud enforcement, microsegmentation, and visibility into shadow AI applications built outside security review.

行业思维：Security teams should把 AI adoption 当作持续控制点，建立模型、云和应用的验证闭环，而不是一次性审批。

Top Cybersecurity News Stories From May 2026 - IllumioGlobal Cybersecurity Outlook 2026 - World Economic Forum
Biotech / Drug Discovery

Biotech AI is moving from task copilots to lab-in-the-loop discovery systems

Biotech coverage describes a sector entering a builder phase: companies are less interested in isolated pilots and more focused on data environments, proprietary models, and closed loops between digital hypotheses and laboratory validation.

Regulatory implications are now part of architecture decisions. Teams that expect AI outputs to support safety, quality, or clinical claims need traceability and validation designed in early.

行业思维：AI drug discovery 的长期壁垒来自专有生物数据、实验验证闭环和可审计流程，而不是通用模型访问权。

The 2026 AI power shift - Drug Discovery NewsAI in Biotech: 2026 Drug Discovery Trends - Ardigen
Energy / AI Data Centers

Power availability is becoming the binding constraint for AI infrastructure

Energy and data-center reporting keeps pointing to the same bottleneck: AI capacity expansion is now constrained by grid-scale power, interconnection timelines, and regional electricity costs.

Hyperscalers are responding by treating energy procurement as strategy, forming direct relationships with power producers and choosing sites around available megawatts rather than only latency or fiber.

行业思维：AI infrastructure 的首要决策应从电力约束、并网流程和本地成本责任开始，再倒推 GPU 采购。
